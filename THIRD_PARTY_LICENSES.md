## VL53L1X ULD driver

`third_party/vl53l1x_uld` holds STMicroelectronics' ultra low power driver for the VL53L1X family
(Copyright (c) 2023 STMicroelectronics, all rights reserved), which also drives the VL53L1CB on this board.
It is licensed under the BSD-3-Clause licence; the licence text is in `third_party/vl53l1x_uld/LICENSE.txt`.

Changes made by Ether Form Inc.: `src/vl53l1_platform.c` is our own implementation of the platform layer
(MIT; it was a stub that did not implement the multi-byte reads and writes) and
`include/vl53l1_platform.h` gained `VL53L1_PlatformSetI2C()`. In `src/VL53L1X_api.c` the default configuration
table sets bit 0 of registers 0x2E and 0x2F, selecting I/O pulled up at AVDD (2.8 V on this board) instead of 1.8 V. The rest is as supplied by ST.
